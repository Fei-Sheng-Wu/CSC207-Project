### 1. For each Principle of Universal Design, explain which features of your program adhere to that principle.

#### Equitable Use

Suitable attempts to comply with the "Equitable Use" principle by targeting a common issue that is applicable to most people's everyday lives. The usage of Suitable is not directed toward any particular demographic but is rather universal.

#### Flexibility in Use

Suitable attempts to comply with the "Flexibility in Use" principle by allowing users to input their own clothing items with high flexibility, allowing for completely arbitrary inputs for attributes associated with each clothing item. Accordingly, Suitable can hence be applied to any case the user desires.

#### Simple and Intuitive Use

Suitable attempts to comply with the "Simple and Intuitive Use" principle by following an extremely simple user interface and easy-to-understand workflow to manage one's wardrobe and attain an outfit recommendation. Suitable uses standard controls whenever possible to reduce the complexity of its usage.

#### Perceptible Information

Suitable attempts to comply with the "Perceptible Information" principle by avoiding reliance on information that is inaccessible to certain demographics. For example, Suitable does not rely on colors but rather distinctive icons for the type categorization of clothing items. In addition, Suitable provides a high-contrast UI mode for people with related disabilities.

#### Tolerance for Error

Suitable attempts to comply with the "Tolerance for Error" principle by implementing strict input checks and validations to avoid application crashes from user-caused errors. Instead, Suitable is able to handle failures gracefully and, in some cases, use corresponding prompts to alert users.

#### Low Physical Effort

Suitable attempts to comply with the "Low Physical Effort" principle by making every action as easy as possible, minimizing the requirement for repetitive physical effort in using the application. The usage of Suitable is designed to be fluent and efficient.

#### Size and Space for Approach and Use

Suitable attempts to comply with the "Size and Space for Approach and Use" principle by providing appropriate spacing between UI controls for ease of use. However, Suitable can also improve on this aspect in the future by implementing additional features that adjust the size of controls to accommodate certain disabilities.

### 2. Write a paragraph about who you would market your program towards, if you were to sell or license your program to customers.

Suitable is mostly marketed toward fashion-centered people who are more attentive to their everyday outfits. The fundamental idea of Suitable's ability to provide context-aware outfit recommendations eases the process of dressing up and reduces the time to do so. In addition, Suitable is also applicable to the working industry who do not have as much time every day for outfit picking, as well as ones who struggle with this very task. At its core, Suitable resolves a crucial problem that is present in most people's everyday lives, hence making it relatable and usable by a lot of demographics beyond our specific targets.

### 3. Write a paragraph about whether or not your program is less likely to be used by certain demographics.

It is likely that Suitable may not be favored by people with certain physical disabilities that severely limit their choice of clothing. Since they do not have the freedom in arbitrary outfit curation, Suitable is less sound to these demographics. However, the high flexibility in Suitable may also allow for customized recommendations that work in one's own case. Hence, whether one rejects the idea of Suitable solely depends on the attractiveness of outfit curation to them.
